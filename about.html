About Us

Sichuan Fukangsi Electric Power Technology Co., Ltd. — genset assembly, sales, rental and on-site repair & maintenance service from Chengdu, China.

Company Profile

Sichuan Fukangsi Electric Power Technology Co., Ltd., based in Chengdu, China, supplies diesel generator sets to industrial and commercial customers.

Core business: genset assembly, sales, rental, and on-site repair & maintenance service. The product range covers land diesel gensets from 3 to 3,000 kW, trailer-mounted mobile generators from 15 to 600 kW, and marine emergency gensets from 12 to 1,000 kW, including silent, high-voltage, parallel-operation and gas-fueled variants.

Sets are assembled on Cummins, Weichai, Shangchai, Jichai and Yuchai engine platforms, among others. Engineering and production follow CAD / CAE / CAM methods.
