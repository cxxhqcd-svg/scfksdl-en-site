1. Gensets

A general genset line for main and standby power across commercial and industrial sites. Built on Tongchai (Shanghai Paou), Huachai Deutz, SDSTF Daewoo and Wuxi Power engines.

Tongchai (Shanghai Paou) Gensets

Power range: 100–650 kW

Overview

Gensets built on Nantong diesel engines of the Tongchai 135/138 series (branded Shanghai Paou; engine plant established 1958). The 135/138 series uses combustion technology licensed from AVL, improving cold startability and fuel economy; large models carry monitoring instruments with four protection functions and remote-control capability.

Key Features

  • Four-stroke, water-cooled, direct injection, turbocharged, closed-circuit fan cooling, 1,500 rpm
  • Integral crankshaft and monobloc cylinder block, interchangeable with legacy 135-series engines
  • Recessed combustion chamber meets applicable emission indicators
  • Optimized lubrication and cooling systems
  • High-altitude capability with power derating below 3%

Applications

Oil fields, mines, factories and institutions; main and standby power.

Huachai Deutz Gensets

Deutz air-cooled & water-cooled engine platforms

Overview

Huachai introduced Deutz air-cooled diesel engines in 1979 (B/F413F), followed by B/F513F (1988), B/F513L (1992), the BFM1015 water-cooled series (2002) and the TCD2015 Euro-III engines (2005). Huachai also developed the HC4132 and HC6132 series on the 1015 platform. More than one hundred engineers have been trained at Deutz in Germany.

Key Features

  • Air-cooled option: compact overall package, wide climate tolerance, fewer cooling-system failures, easy maintenance, rapid warm-up and lower cylinder wear
  • Water-cooled option covers BFM1015 / TCD2015
  • Technical cooperation with universities and research institutes
  • Note: air-cooled engines have higher operating noise than water-cooled types

Applications

General and standby power where compactness and climate adaptability are required.
